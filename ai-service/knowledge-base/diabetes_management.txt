CLINICAL PRACTICE GUIDELINE: TYPE 2 DIABETES MELLITUS MANAGEMENT

1. Diagnostic Criteria
- Fasting Plasma Glucose (FPG) >= 126 mg/dL (7.0 mmol/L)
- 2-hour Post-Prandial Glucose >= 200 mg/dL (11.1 mmol/L)
- Glycated Hemoglobin (HbA1c) >= 6.5% (48 mmol/mol)

2. First-Line Pharmacotherapy
- Metformin: Initial dose 500mg once or twice daily with meals. Titrate gradually to max 2000mg daily.
  - Caution: Check renal function (eGFR < 30 mL/min/1.73m2 is absolute contraindication).
  - Side Effects: Gastrointestinal upset, vitamin B12 deficiency with long-term use.
- Second-Line Add-ons (if HbA1c remains above target after 3 months):
  - SGLT2 Inhibitors (e.g., Empagliflozin 10mg daily, Dapagliflozin 10mg daily): Proven cardiovascular and renal protection.
  - GLP-1 Receptor Agonists (e.g., Semaglutide, Liraglutide): Weight reduction benefit.
  - Sulfonylureas (e.g., Glimepiride 1-4mg daily, Gliclazide 30-120mg daily): Monitor closely for hypoglycemia.

3. Hypoglycemia Management & Warning Signs
- Hypoglycemia is defined as blood glucose < 70 mg/dL (3.9 mmol/L).
- Symptoms: Tremors, sweating, palpitations, confusion, dizziness.
- Treatment: Rule of 15 (15g fast-acting carbohydrate, recheck glucose in 15 minutes).
